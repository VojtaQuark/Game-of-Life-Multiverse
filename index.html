Rules & notation

Life-like rules: every cell has 8 neighbors. B = neighbor counts that create a dead cell; S = counts that keep a live cell alive. Conway is B3/S23.

Pn = period n · Δ(x,y) = movement per period · ⁻¹ = alive infinity with finite dead exceptions · S∅ = no live-cell survival.

Outcome labels

Dead · Still · Oscillating Pn · Moving Pn Δ(x,y) · Likely growing · Undecided · All alive.
